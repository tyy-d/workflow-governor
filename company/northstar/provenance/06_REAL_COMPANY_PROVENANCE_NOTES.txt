NORTHSTAR 06 — REAL-COMPANY / PUBLIC-SOURCE PROVENANCE NOTES
==============================================================

Northstar Regional Markets is fictional. These sources supplied process ideas only; Northstar names, thresholds, forms, statuses, deadlines, examples, and exception logic are original.

GFSI (current recognised-programme pages)
- Borrowed concept: GFSI recognises certification programmes/versions for specific scopes; certification bodies certify sites.
- Northstar adaptation: R3 food facilities normally need a current certificate under a currently recognised programme/version and matching scope, or an approved alternative audit plan.

Sysco
- Borrowed concept: initial supplier-facility approval, detailed specifications, ongoing monitoring, annual independent food-safety certification, and more frequent review for higher-risk suppliers.
- Northstar adaptation: FQR facility approval plus annual R3 status review and risk-triggered audits/testing.

Target
- Borrowed concept: factory assessment, documented manufacturing/quality systems, risk-based production management, and independent third-party testing across the owned-brand lifecycle.
- Northstar adaptation: QVP testing plans, competent/independent labs, and testing tied to private-label release gates.

UNFI
- Borrowed concept: onboarding asks for recent third-party food-safety audit evidence and GMP operation; supplier terms reserve audit/corrective-action rights.
- Northstar adaptation: audit evidence in the FQR and formal CAP/status consequences.

Family Dollar / Dollar Tree
- Borrowed concept: retailer-controlled product testing, facility documentation, direct-import food verification, and pre-shipment quality release.
- Northstar adaptation: lot-linked COA/testing requirements and technical release controls without copying Family Dollar thresholds.

FDA — Preventive Controls / FSVP / Food Defense / Traceability
- Borrowed concept: hazard analysis, preventive controls, supplier verification via audits/testing/record review, recall/traceability readiness, corrective action, and food-defense planning where applicable.
- Northstar adaptation: risk-based evidence requirements and a 4-hour Northstar Traceability Challenge.
- Current-date note: FDA states Food Traceability Rule enforcement will not begin before July 20, 2028; Northstar's present traceability threshold is company policy, not that federal compliance date.

Key Northstar-original numbers in 06
- Alternative audit: >=85/100 and zero Critical findings.
- FQR reassessment: R3 every 12 months; approval-controlled R2 every 24 months.
- Traceability Challenge: 4 hours.
- Critical CAP package: 5 business days; Major: 10; Minor: 20.
- Normal action completion: 30 calendar days Critical/Major; 60 Minor.
- Certification-continuity exception: maximum 45 calendar days after prior certificate expiry.
