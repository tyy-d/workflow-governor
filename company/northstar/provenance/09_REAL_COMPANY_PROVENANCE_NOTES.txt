NORTHSTAR FILE 09 — REAL-COMPANY PROVENANCE NOTES
===================================================
Northstar is fictional. These sources influenced process design only; Northstar names, deadlines, tolerances, statuses, and controls are original.

DOLLAR TREE / FAMILY DOLLAR
- Borrowed: merchandise suppliers receiving POs and sending invoices electronically; portal-or-EDI model; connectivity testing; structured PO/invoice validation and supplier visibility.
- Northstar adaptation: VendorLink + approved EDI/API channels, with one authoritative transaction/status record and original outage/backfill rules.

US FOODS
- Borrowed: supplier self-service for invoice submission, claims/documentation, and payment-management visibility.
- Northstar adaptation: invoice, IER, payment-status, remittance, and supporting-document workflows in VendorLink.

SYSCO
- Borrowed: supplier portals exposing PO, invoice, payment, evidence, and inquiry status.
- Northstar adaptation: linked transaction history and role-based exception ownership without creating a second portal.

UNFI
- Borrowed: PO terms remain controlling over supplier invoice language; payment timing linked to invoice/receipt events; deductions and offsets handled within AP settlement.
- Northstar adaptation: three-way match, Invoice Eligible Date, default Net 30, and separate chargeback/appeal treatment in file 10.
