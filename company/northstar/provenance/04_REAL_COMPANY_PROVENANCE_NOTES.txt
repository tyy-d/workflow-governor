NORTHSTAR 04 — SHORT PROVENANCE MAP
===================================
Northstar Regional Markets is fictional. The sources below supplied process ideas only; Northstar names, thresholds, approvals, forms, metrics, and examples are original.

FAMILY DOLLAR / DOLLAR TREE
- Borrowed concept: standardized master-carton markings, pack/inner distinctions, handling marks, buyer-approved deviations, and consequences for bad markings.
- Northstar adaptation: APLR-controlled Master Case fields, case-pack approval, handling symbols, and VendorLink exception workflow.

DOLLAR TREE UPC BASICS
- Borrowed concept: barcode print quality, quiet zones, placement, color/contrast, and pre-shipment scan verification.
- Northstar adaptation: Consumer GTIN controls plus 10-of-10 decode test and Northstar minimum grade 2.0/C where verifier grading is available.

GS1 US
- Borrowed concept: GTIN packaging hierarchy, GTIN-14 for shipping cases, GS1-128 logistics labels, and SSCC as a unique logistics-unit identifier.
- Northstar adaptation: unique SSCC on non-exempt DC pallets and APLR-controlled consumer/case/logistics identifiers.

FDA / USDA-FSIS
- Borrowed concept: food labels have regulator-specific mandatory identity, quantity, ingredients/allergens, nutrition, handling, inspection, and business information.
- Northstar adaptation: Q&R label review sits on top of applicable law; Northstar approval is never represented as regulatory approval.

FDA COSMETICS / FTC FPLA / CPSC
- Borrowed concept: identity, net contents, ingredients/warnings/business information and product-specific consumer/tracking warnings vary by jurisdiction/product class.
- Northstar adaptation: one cross-category APLR with category-specific legal review rather than pretending all merchandise follows food rules.

NORTHSTAR-ORIGINAL ITEMS
- Approved Packaging & Label Record (APLR) and Temporary Overlabel Authorization (TOA).
- 30-day branded-item change notice; private-label 60-day PLCR remains controlling.
- >5% case dimension/gross-weight master-data correction trigger.
- 10/10 correct barcode decode rule; 3 Major events in 60 days triggers corrective-action review.
- Critical/Major/Minor packaging-label severity model and Northstar approval matrix.
