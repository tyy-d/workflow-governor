NORTHSTAR 08 — REAL-COMPANY / PUBLIC-SOURCE PROVENANCE NOTES
=============================================================

Purpose: quick trace of ideas borrowed structurally. Northstar wording, thresholds, systems, and penalties are fictional.

FAMILY DOLLAR / DOLLAR TREE
- Borrowed concepts: prepaid vs collect freight; shipment release before movement; separate DC appointment step; BOL identity fields; blocking/bracing; sealed trailer loads; DSD as a distinct transportation path.
- Northstar adaptation: SRR, Ready By rules, its own lead times, incident thresholds, and approval logic.

DOLLAR TREE LOGISTICS
- Borrowed concept: portal shipment-release data feeds transportation planning, while a separate reservation process handles DC appointments.
- Northstar adaptation: VendorLink SRR + existing DockSchedule.

FDA — SANITARY TRANSPORTATION
- Borrowed concepts: sanitary vehicle condition, written temperature/sanitation specifications, pre-cooling, prior-cargo/cleaning information when relevant, carrier procedures/training, and preservation of temperature-control evidence.
- Legal source; Northstar adds stricter company-specific response times and records.

SYSCO
- Borrowed concepts: reefer monitoring/downloads, continuous refrigeration when specified, clean trailers, seal chain-of-custody, status messages, carrier scorecards.
- Northstar adaptation: 15-minute temp interval, 4-business-hour record response, 1-hour critical incident notice, original score targets.

FMCSA
- Borrowed legal concepts: cargo securement, HOS/weight compliance boundary, hazmat shipping papers/placards/compatibility.
- Northstar does not create substitutes for federal transportation law.

KEY NORTHSTAR-ORIGINAL ITEMS
- Shipment Routing Record (SRR)
- 2/3-business-day routing request lead times
- >10% shipment-data update threshold
- $150,000 cargo-liability minimum for Northstar-contracted carriers
- 15-minute R3 temperature-evidence interval
- 4-business-hour temperature-record response
- 1-hour critical / 2-hour major-delay notification
- Northstar carrier-score thresholds and 60-day event escalation
