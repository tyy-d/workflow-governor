NORTHSTAR 07 — SHORT PROVENANCE MAP
=====================================
Northstar is fictional. The items below are process ideas only; Northstar names, systems,
thresholds, approval rules, examples, and consequences are original.

FAMILY DOLLAR — current Vendor Resources + Part 8 Distribution Requirements
- Borrowed concept: explicit DC receiving rules for pallet condition/size, stable loads,
  product grouping, pallet labels, packing-list placement, and receiving identification.
- Northstar adaptation: 48x40 default pallets, Northstar-specific 2,200-lb/66-in limits,
  APLR/SSCC checks, RER evidence, and original hold/escalation rules.

DOLLAR TREE — Logistics + Inbound Shipping Requirements
- Borrowed concept: separate shipment-release/appointment workflow, confirmed appointment
  numbers, BOL/packing data, and measurable arrival expectations.
- Northstar adaptation: DockSchedule; 2-business-day request target; -45/+30 minute normal
  arrival window; 60-minute No Show threshold; Gate Arrival Time as the evidence used by OTIF.

FDA — FSMA Sanitary Transportation rule/guidance
- Borrowed concept: receivers assess sanitary condition and possible temperature abuse;
  potentially unsafe food is not distributed until appropriately evaluated.
- Northstar adaptation: trailer-condition checks, Temperature Hold, expanded sampling, and
  Q&R-controlled disposition.

FDA — 2022 Food Code receiving-temperature concepts
- Borrowed concept: 41°F receiving criterion for refrigerated TCS food and 45°F ambient
  criterion for raw shell eggs, subject to product-specific law.
- Northstar adaptation: DC sampling matrix plus separate company defaults for other
  refrigerated and frozen products.

NOT BORROWED FROM ANY ONE COMPANY
- Hudson Valley / Lehigh Valley DC names and codes
- Northstar DockSchedule and Receiving Exception Record (RER)
- Northstar Receipt Date definition
- all appointment, sampling, pallet-weight/height, damage-event, repeat-event, and retention
  thresholds
- all approval authorities and scenario examples
