NORTHSTAR 02 — REAL-COMPANY / PUBLIC-POLICY PROVENANCE NOTES
============================================================
Purpose: short trace of external concepts used while designing 02_PRODUCT_FRESHNESS.md.
Northstar-specific thresholds, forms, names, approval paths, score bands, and examples are fictional.

FAMILY DOLLAR / DOLLAR TREE
Source: Family Dollar Routing, Shipping, and Packaging Instructions — Freshness Policy.
Borrowed concept:
- retailer can negotiate an item-specific guaranteed shelf-life commitment rather than relying only on total shelf life;
- short-dated product can be rejected despite still being within the manufacturer's total life;
- where multiple expiration dates are grouped together, the least-fresh date can control.
Northstar adaptation:
- "Approved Freshness Profile" (AFP), "Minimum Remaining Life" (MRL), case/lot segregation rules, and Freshness Hold.
- Family Dollar's actual GSL values/penalties were NOT copied.

UNFI
Sources: UNFI Supplier Terms & Conditions (updated July 30, 2026); UNFI UpNext Supplier Inquiry.
Borrowed concept:
- percentage-of-total-shelf-life requirement at distribution-center receipt;
- supplier responsibility for meeting that shelf-life requirement;
- structured capture of shelf life at production plus shipping/selling temperature.
Northstar adaptation:
- AFP stores DTSL, MRL, storage condition, date-code meaning, and decode instructions.
- Northstar's 60%/65%/70% category defaults are original; UNFI's 75% rule was NOT copied.

FDA
Source: FDA Food Code and retail food-safety guidance.
Borrowed concept:
- cold holding and food-safety temperature control are distinct from commercial quality/shelf-life policy.
Northstar adaptation:
- 02 defines freshness semantics but reserves receiving-temperature execution for files 07/08.

USDA FSIS
Source: Food Product Dating.
Borrowed concept:
- distinction between open calendar dates and coded/closed dates;
- most federal food date labels are quality indicators, not universal safety deadlines;
- specific legal exceptions exist (for example infant formula under FDA requirements).
Northstar adaptation:
- explicit legal-vs-company-policy distinction and derived-date/decode workflow.
