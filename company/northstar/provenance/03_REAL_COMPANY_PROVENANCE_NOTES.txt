NORTHSTAR 03 — REAL-COMPANY PROVENANCE NOTES
==============================================
Purpose: concise record of concepts adapted for 03_PRIVATE_LABEL_PRODUCTS.md.
Northstar names, forms, deadlines, statuses, approval rules, and examples are fictional and original.

FAMILY DOLLAR / DOLLAR TREE
Source: public Family Dollar Quality & Regulatory Compliance Manual (v2.4, 2022).
Borrowed concepts:
- Private-brand products receive stronger quality/regulatory controls than ordinary resale items.
- Manufacturing locations are disclosed/approved before production; unauthorized subcontracting is serious.
- Final product specifications are approved before production/PO commitment.
- Pre-production samples and first-production/production samples can be used as conformity references.
- Product/specification changes can trigger approval and re-testing.
Northstar adaptation:
- Created the PLTF, APS, PPS, FPL, PLCR, 10-business-day PPS lead time, and 60-day material-change rule.

TARGET
Sources: current Product Safety & Quality Assurance; Food Safety Quality & Regulatory; Standards of Vendor Engagement; Traceability & Transparency.
Borrowed concepts:
- Owned-brand controls span the full product lifecycle.
- Factories are assessed before production and production locations must be disclosed.
- Detailed food specifications, risk-based production readiness, third-party testing, allergen programs, and lot traceability are central controls.
Northstar adaptation:
- Created Production Authorized / Shipping Authorized gates, annual PLTF attestation, and risk-based first-production release.

SYSCO
Source: current Quality Assurance page.
Borrowed concepts:
- Distributor-brand food is evaluated against detailed product specifications.
- Brand suppliers/facilities undergo initial and ongoing QA oversight.
Northstar adaptation:
- Reinforced APS-based conformity and approved-site controls for private-label food.

FDA
Sources: How to Start a Food Business; Food Allergies.
Borrowed concepts:
- Food manufacturers/processors/distributors retain legal responsibility for safe, sanitary, correctly labeled food.
- Allergen preventive controls are part of applicable food-manufacturing requirements.
Northstar adaptation:
- Private-label policy cannot waive legal/safety/allergen obligations; detailed regulatory mechanics are deferred to file 06.

CPSC
Sources: General Use Products: Certification and Testing; Third Party Testing Guidance.
Borrowed concepts:
- Some regulated consumer products require testing/certification, with different legal rules for general-use and children's products.
- Material changes can matter to continued compliance.
Northstar adaptation:
- Northstar uses broader risk-based private-label verification while leaving legally required certificates/testing to the applicable product category and file 06.

KEY POINT
Northstar did NOT copy real-company thresholds. The PLTF/APS model, 10-business-day PPS lead time, 2-business-day FPL evidence deadline, 60-day planned-change notice, 12-month attestation, 15-day overdue production block, exception matrix, and record-retention trigger are Northstar-original test-environment rules.
