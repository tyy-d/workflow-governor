CANONICAL FACTS ESTABLISHED IN FILE 01
Purchase-order authority / response
- Commercial PO is authoritative only when transmitted through VendorLink or an approved electronic connection and identified by PO number + revision.
- Forecasts, emails, verbal commitments, and sample requests are not commercial POs.
- Standard new PO/material revision acknowledgement: within 1 Northstar business day; if earliest required performance date is <3 business days away: within 4 business hours.
- Acknowledgement line statuses: Accepted / Change Requested / Rejected. Requested vendor changes do not modify a PO until Northstar issues a revision. Shipping without acknowledgement is treated as acceptance but remains a process violation.
- Vendor shortage/delay notice: within 1 business day of discovery and, when foreseeable, at least 2 business days before the affected window.

PO quantity / performance logic
- Unauthorized substitutions and over-shipments prohibited; split shipments require prior approval unless PO explicitly permits.
- Net Ordered Units = latest authorized units minus eligible Northstar cancellations.
- Northstar cancellation reduces denominator only if transmitted before earlier of vendor shipment/formal pickup readiness OR start of controlling window. Vendor-caused cancellations remain in denominator.
- On-Time Unit Rate = compliant on-time units / Net Ordered Units.
- Fill Rate = compliant units received by final close / Net Ordered Units. Numerators capped at ordered units.
- PO final close: 5 calendar days after end of controlling delivery window unless all lines close earlier. Late-but-before-close units may improve Fill Rate, not On-Time Unit Rate.
- PO On Time >=98.0%; In Full >=98.0%; OTIF Pass requires both.
- Monthly target: OTIF Pass Rate >=95.0%; weighted Fill Rate >=98.5%.
- <5 eligible POs in a month = informational scorecard only, except severe/Critical PO events.

Score bands / escalation
- Meets Standard: OTIF >=95.0% AND fill >=98.5%.
- Watch: OTIF 90.0-94.99% OR fill 96.0-98.49%.
- Corrective: OTIF <90.0% OR fill <96.0%, or 2 consecutive Watch months.
- Corrective status requires CAP within 10 business days.
- 2 consecutive Corrective months or 3 Corrective months in rolling 6 months triggers formal review; Vendor Compliance Manager + responsible Procurement manager may Restrict affected vendor/item/lane.
- Critical PO: >20% of Net Ordered Units not on time, or zero-delivery/no-ready without timely notice -> immediate performance review.

Transportation responsibility boundary
- Vendor-prepaid: vendor measured on compliant arrival within controlling delivery/appointment window.
- Northstar-managed pickup: vendor measured on product fully staged/conforming/documented/available by PO Ready By deadline; Northstar-carrier delay does not make vendor late if readiness is credibly evidenced.
- DSD: vendor measured on compliant store delivery within controlling window.
- Detailed appointment/receiving/carrier/routing rules remain reserved for files 07 and 08.

Exceptions / evidence
- New recurring record: Performance Exception Record (PER) in VendorLink.
- PER request normally due within 3 business days after affected event.
- Approvals: Northstar PO/data error = Vendor Compliance Manager + Procurement manager; Northstar-managed transport/appointment failure = Logistics Manager; material commercial quantity/date exception = Procurement manager + Vendor Compliance Manager; safety/regulatory = Director Q&R; post-scorecard correction = Vendor Compliance Manager + manager owning evidence.
- PER cannot waive law, authorize known unsafe product, or override Q&R safety hold.
- PO/performance records retained 7 years after PO final close unless longer retention applies.

CANONICAL FACTS ESTABLISHED IN FILE 02
Freshness data model
- Item-level authoritative record: Approved Freshness Profile (AFP) in VendorLink.
- AFP includes DTSL, Shelf-Life Start Event, date-code type/meaning, decode instructions, storage condition, MRL, lot format, specification, and approvals.
- No routine PO for a freshness-controlled item without an approved AFP.
- Remaining Life (RL) = controlling End-of-Life Date minus Northstar receipt date; receipt date is day 0.
- When grouped product contains multiple dates, least remaining life controls unless date groups can be reliably segregated and handled separately.
- Manufacture/pack code + DTSL may be used to derive ELD only when the AFP contains an approved decode method. Undecodable product goes on Freshness Hold.

Default MRL policy (AFP always controls)
- Shelf-stable food DTSL >180 days: 70% remaining.
- Shelf-stable food DTSL 91-180 days: 70% remaining.
- Shelf-stable food DTSL <=90 days: 65% remaining.
- Refrigerated packaged food: 65% remaining.
- Frozen food: 70% remaining.
- Highly perishable fresh categories: item-specific AFP mandatory; no category default.
- Dated/stability-controlled non-food: 60% remaining unless item-specific.
- Percentage MRL rounds up to next whole day; if AFP has percentage + absolute days, greater requirement controls.

Freshness controls / performance
- Short-dated = not expired but below MRL; ordinarily a commercial freshness failure, not automatically a safety failure.
- Expired cases are never freshness-compliant; discovery of an expired case places affected lot on Q&R hold pending scope review.
- Freshness Compliance Rate = compliant cases / freshness-eligible cases. Monthly target >=99.5%.
- <98.0%, two consecutive Freshness Watch months, expired product, or deliberate date-code manipulation triggers corrective-action review.
- <100 eligible cases/month = informational percentage, but Critical Freshness Events still escalate.
- Freshness is scored separately from OTIF.

Freshness changes / exceptions
- Planned DTSL reduction, shelf-life start change, date-meaning change, or storage-condition change: VendorLink submission >=45 calendar days before affected product ships.
- Unplanned condition potentially invalidating approved shelf life: notify Q&R + Vendor Compliance within 1 business day of discovery and before further affected shipment where possible.
- Freshness Exception (FE) must be lot/quantity-specific and approved before shipment where possible; blanket/no-expiry FEs invalid.
- R1/R2 non-safety short-date FE: Merchandising Manager + Vendor Compliance Manager.
- R3 non-safety short-date FE: Merchandising Manager + Director, Quality & Regulatory.
- Promotion/material-supply exception also requires Procurement Manager.
- No FE may waive law, knowingly unsafe distribution, lost shelf-life validation, or altered/falsified date codes.

- 06 owns shelf-life validation studies/testing and safety-quality investigation.
- 07 owns physical receipt timestamp, inspection, temperature evidence, segregation, and execution of holds/rejections.
- 08 owns in-transit cold-chain/carrier controls.

Private-label ownership / data model
- Private-label foods remain R3 Enhanced Food/Safety under file 00; non-food private label keeps its Q&R-assigned tier.

- New controlled records/terms: Pre-Production Sample (PPS), First Production Lot (FPL), Private Label Change Request (PLCR).

Production / site controls
- Private-label production only at the Approved Manufacturing Site recorded for the active PLTF version.
- Unauthorized finished-goods subcontracting is a Critical Private Label Deviation and triggers hold + vendor-status review.
- New item/new site/Q&R-designated material change: PPS due >=10 Northstar business days before planned production unless a longer category lead time applies.
- Production may begin only when VendorLink shows Production Authorized for that PLTF version/site.
- Production Authorized / Shipping Authorized are technical release gates and do not replace a commercial PO or PO revision under file 01.
- FPL evidence due within 2 business days after lot completion.
- R3 private-label food and items marked Hold for First-Production Release cannot ship until VendorLink shows Shipping Authorized.

Change / recertification controls
- Planned Material Change: PLCR >=60 calendar days before planned production; approval required before changed production. Earlier deadlines in another policy still control.
- Unplanned possible material change/deviation: notify Q&R + Vendor Compliance within 1 business day of discovery and before further affected shipment where reasonably possible.
- Zero commercial tolerance for unauthorized allergen, legal identity/composition, site, substantiated claim, critical safety process/component, required regulatory certification, Northstar mark, or mandatory warning changes.
- Active private-label items require Annual Technical Attestation every 12 months. VendorLink opens 30 days before anniversary.
- Missing at anniversary -> Technical Review Due; still incomplete 15 calendar days later -> new PO production cannot begin until Vendor Compliance + Q&R clearance.

Exception / record controls
- Emergency private-label change uses PLCR with Emergency flag; blanket/no-expiry exceptions invalid.

Import timing / deterministic thresholds
- New imported item/source ICR: complete >=15 Northstar business days before first planned foreign cargo-ready date.

Regulatory / source controls

- Covered CPSC imported consumer products require applicable CPC/GCC support and required eFiling; current eFiling effective date noted in file 05 is July 8, 2026.

CANONICAL FACTS ESTABLISHED IN FILE 06
Quality / facility data model
- New authoritative facility record: Facility Quality Record (FQR) in VendorLink.
- Facility quality statuses: Approved / Conditional / Restricted / Disapproved. These are separate from NVID vendor statuses in file 00.
- New item/product-family testing record: Quality Verification Plan (QVP).
- New defined records/terms: Certificate of Analysis (COA), Critical Quality Event (CQE), Corrective Action Plan (CAP), Traceability Challenge.
- Q&R owns technical facility approval, testing requirements, regulatory evidence, safety holds, and quality finding severity.

R3 certification / audit controls
- R3 food default: current certificate under a currently GFSI-recognised certification programme/version for the relevant scope, issued through the appropriate certification system, OR Director-Q&R-approved alternative audit plan.
- GFSI recognition is of programmes/scopes, not direct site certification.
- Alternative audit acceptance: >=85/100 where scored, zero Critical findings, no unresolved unsafe/illegal condition, accepted CAP for Major findings.
- Alternative audit at least every 12 months unless Q&R sets shorter.
- FQR reassessment: R3 at least every 12 months; approval-controlled R2 every 24 months; event-driven review may occur sooner.
- Certification-continuity exception: Director Q&R only; pre-approved; no open Critical finding; scheduled recertification/alternative audit; normally expires <=45 calendar days after prior certificate expiry.

Testing / COA controls
- QVP defines test, specification/limit, method, sample source, frequency, laboratory, and release condition.
- Safety/regulatory testing normally uses an independent ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory for an appropriate scope when reasonably available; Q&R may accept a justified competent alternative.
- Lot-release COA must identify supplier/facility, item, lot, dates, method, limit/specification, actual result/units, and responsible lab/quality function.
- A COA from another lot is invalid unless QVP explicitly approves a representative-lot strategy.
- QVP testing may be part of file 03 private-label technical release and does not replace Production Authorized/Shipping Authorized.

Traceability / FSVP
- R3 foods and Q&R-designated R2 items must support lot-level traceability.
- Northstar Traceability Challenge response deadline: 4 hours; failure to complete in 4 hours = Major, inability to bound affected lot population or deliberate falsification = Critical.
- Northstar's 4-hour traceability rule is company policy. FDA Food Traceability Rule enforcement is not expected before July 20, 2028 as of file 06 research date.
- When Northstar is FSVP importer, Q&R oversees the applicable risk evaluation/verification; reevaluation at least every 3 years or sooner when legally triggered by new hazard/supplier-performance information.

Quality-event notification / CAP
- Active recall, stop-sale/stop-distribution, credible serious safety hazard, material government seizure/refusal, or confirmed falsified safety evidence: notify Q&R + Vendor Compliance within 4 business hours.
- Other material regulatory notice, failed safety-critical test, certification suspension/withdrawal, or likely affected Northstar product: within 1 business day.
- Critical finding: immediate containment; containment summary 1 business day; CAP/root-cause package 5 business days.
- Major CAP: 10 business days. Minor CAP when required: 20 business days.
- Normal action completion: Critical/Major 30 calendar days; Minor 60 calendar days, unless approved extension with interim controls.
- Disapproved facility may not produce new Northstar product.
- Quality/compliance records retained 7 years after later of last Northstar receipt from affected facility or affected-item retirement unless longer rule applies.

Receipt / appointment evidence
- Gate Arrival Time = first presentation at correct DC gate with sufficient shipment identity.
- Northstar Receipt Date for an ultimately unloaded live delivery = local calendar date of Gate Arrival; accepted drop = date Northstar accepts trailer custody; turned-away pre-unload load has no Receipt Date. File 02 uses this receipt date as day 0.
- Live unload normally requires confirmed DockSchedule appointment. Prepaid request target >=2 Northstar business days before planned arrival. Same-day requires DC Receiving Manager capacity approval.

- Onsite receiving/document check-in expected within 15 minutes after gate entry unless directed otherwise.

Pallet / receiving controls
- Default pallet: 48x40 inch four-way; max gross pallet weight 2,200 lb; max loaded height 66 inches including pallet; no overhang; structurally sound/clean. APLR/PO/lane exception may override.
- Floor-load requires PO/lane authorization or prior Logistics/DC approval; operational acceptance still creates RER when unauthorized.
- Unauthorized substitutions are not receivable as ordered item; unordered/overage product cannot enter saleable inventory without PO revision/approved exception.
- Visible damage >=5 cases OR >=1.0% of presented cases, whichever threshold is reached first, requires RER before receipt close; safety-sensitive damage/contamination triggers RER/hold regardless of quantity.

Freshness / temperature receiving
- Freshness-controlled receipt sampling default: >=3 cases per item/lot; AFP/file 02 remains authoritative.
- R3 temperature-controlled food: ambient/setpoint when available + >=3 spatially separated product-temperature readings. R2 temperature-controlled: >=2 product readings unless stricter QVP/AFP.
- Northstar fallback receiving defaults when no stricter item/legal spec: refrigerated TCS <=41°F; raw shell eggs refrigerated-equipment ambient <=45°F; other refrigerated quality-controlled food <=45°F; frozen hard-frozen/no material thaw-refreeze and product <=10°F.
- Any out-of-spec temp or credible cold-chain concern -> Temperature Hold; expand to >=6 product readings when safely practicable; Q&R controls disposition. Do not average away an out-of-spec reading.

Hold / escalation / performance
- Seal mismatch/break on R3 or safety-sensitive load, infestation/material contamination, temperature failure, missing import release/readiness, expired product, or active Q&R hold normally prevents saleable release.
- DC Receiving Manager may approve temporary operational dock/staging/unloading exceptions; Logistics Manager approves recurring appointment/lane exceptions; Q&R controls safety/temperature/regulatory/technical hold release.
- Receiving corrective-action review trigger: 3 Major receiving events in rolling 60 days; or 2 Late Arrival/No Show events in 30 days on a lane with >=4 scheduled deliveries; any Critical contamination/infestation/falsification/safety event also escalates.
- If file 06 classifies the issue as a quality finding, file 06 CAP deadlines control.
- Receiving/RER records retained 7 years after receipt close unless longer rule applies.

Cross-file boundaries added by 07
- 08 owns detailed carrier routing, sanitation, seal and cold-chain execution; 07 owns what DC physically observes/records at receipt.
- 09 owns detailed ASN/electronic transmission mechanics; 07 consumes available ASN/SSCC data for receipt validation.
- 10 owns dollar chargebacks, returns/disposition cost recovery, and appeals; 07 establishes receiving facts but no dollar amount.


CANONICAL FACTS ESTABLISHED IN FILE 08
=======================================
Transportation ownership / data model
- New authoritative shipment-level transportation record: Shipment Routing Record (SRR) in VendorLink.
- Freight paths: Vendor-Prepaid; Northstar-Managed Pickup; DSD; NDI domestic handoff after lawful U.S. release.
- Logistics owns routing/carrier assignment/transport operating standards; Q&R owns food-safety/temperature/sanitation disposition; DC Operations owns physical receiving evidence.
- SRR does not replace PO, ASN, DockSchedule, APLR, AFP, ICR, QVP, or private-label release.

Routing / readiness
- Northstar-Managed complete SRR request lead time: standard dry LTL >=2 Northstar business days before Ready By; dry TL, refrigerated/frozen TL, regulated hazmat, and NDI domestic handoff >=3 business days.
- Late routing request does not change PO Ready By.
- Northstar-Managed freight may not use a vendor-selected substitute carrier without written Logistics approval.
- SRR shipment data must be updated before pickup for changes to equipment type, hazmat status, temperature requirement, pickup address, or ship date; >10% change in expected gross weight or pallet/carton count also requires update when known.
- Northstar-Managed readiness means conforming/released product at correct origin, packed/labeled/documented/staged and available by Ready By; credible evidence may include warehouse timestamps, staged-freight records/photos, loader records, or contemporaneous carrier communication.
- Northstar-carrier delay after credible timely readiness remains Northstar-controlled under file 01 responsibility logic.

Carrier / BOL / insurance
- Northstar-contracted motor carrier minimums: $1M CSL auto liability (preserves file 00) + $150,000 cargo liability per occurrence unless contract requires more.
- BOL/equivalent required for every DC shipment; includes shipper/origin, destination, carrier/trailer, freight term, PO, SRR if managed pickup, pallet/carton count/weight, sufficient item identity, seal when required, temperature instruction, and legally required hazmat information.
- Carriers under government order prohibiting operation may not carry Northstar-controlled freight.
- Northstar policy never requires HOS/weight/hazmat-law violation to meet schedule.

Food transport / seals / temperature
- Food equipment must be suitable, clean, sanitary, pest/odor/contaminant free; prior-cargo/cleaning evidence required when needed for suitability.
- All closed truckload shipments to a Northstar DC are sealed at origin unless mode requires intermediate access; authorized LTL/multi-stop loads use chain-of-custody controls.
- R3/safety-sensitive Seal Event records include old seal, reason, time/location, actor/authority, and replacement seal. Unexplained R3 seal mismatch/break normally triggers file 07 receiving hold.
- R3 refrigerated/frozen TL is pre-cooled to specified operating condition and uses continuous refrigeration unless written Q&R/product instruction permits another method.
- R3 temperature evidence interval <=15 minutes when equipment is capable; requested reefer/equivalent record due within 4 business hours.
- Known temp excursion, refrigeration failure, accidental shutdown, or suspected logger tampering -> Logistics + Q&R notice within 1 hour.
- File 07 receiving temperatures are not substitute transit setpoints.

Transportation incidents / performance
- Collision, theft/hijack attempt, unexplained seal breach, temperature-control failure, contamination, government impoundment, or cargo spill: Logistics notice within 1 hour.
- Other event expected to delay pickup/delivery >2 hours: notice within 2 hours of learning.
- Supporting incident evidence added to SRR within 1 Northstar business day when reasonably available.
- Northstar-contracted carrier scorecard: tender acceptance >=94%; on-time pickup >=95%; required status reporting >=97%; required temperature-record availability 100%.
- <10 eligible loads/month = informational percentage unless Critical event.
- Two consecutive months below an applicable carrier standard or any Critical Transportation Event -> Logistics review.
- Three Major Transportation Events for same vendor/carrier in rolling 60 days -> corrective-action review; transportation CAP due in 10 business days when file 06 Q&R CAP timing does not apply.
- Transportation records retained 7 years after final delivery or later claim/incident closure unless longer rule applies.

CANONICAL FACTS ESTABLISHED IN FILE 09
=======================================
VendorLink / transaction data model
- Northstar VendorLink remains the vendor-facing system of record for transaction status, supporting documents, exceptions, and payment visibility; approved EDI/API connections are alternate transmission channels, not separate authorities.
- Core transaction records: PO/PO Revision; PO Acknowledgment; ASN; Invoice; Credit Memo; Remittance Advice. File 01 continues to own PO commercial authority and acknowledgement timing.
- New controlled record: Invoice Exception Record (IER) in VendorLink.

ASN controls
- ASN required for DC-bound merchandise unless VendorLink marks the lane/vendor ASN Exempt.
- ASN must be accepted before arrival, normally no later than physical departure and >=4 hours before confirmed DC appointment when transit time permits; transit <4 hours requires transmission before departure.
- ASN links NVID, PO/revision, origin/destination, shipment/carrier/BOL references, pallet/carton quantities, item/line quantities, required SSCCs, and lot/date/temp data when applicable.
- Latest accepted ASN controls electronic receiving expectations but cannot authorize over-shipment, substitution, destination, or PO-quantity changes.

Invoice / matching controls
- Merchandise invoice requires NVID, unique invoice number, invoice date, PO, line/item, quantity, unit price, line extension, authorized separate charges, currency, and total.
- Invoice may be sent after shipment departure; recommended within 2 business days; required no later than 10 calendar days after Northstar Receipt Date unless written terms differ.
- Duplicate key: same NVID + invoice number cannot coexist as active/paid invoice.
- Merchandise three-way match compares latest authorized PO, Northstar receipt, and invoice.
- Auto-match requires cumulative invoiced qty <= accepted receipt qty; unit price variance within BOTH $1.00 per invoice line and 0.25% of PO line value; unauthorized freight/tax/handling/deposit/misc charge = $0; currency/UOM match PO. Tolerance enables automation and does not authorize price changes.
- VendorLink invoice statuses: Received / Validation Failed / Match Pending / Exception Review / Approved for Payment / Scheduled / Paid / Rejected / Void.

Payment / exception controls
- Default Northstar merchandise term = Net 30 unless signed agreement/PO states otherwise.
- DC-merchandise Invoice Eligible Date (IED) = later of valid invoice receipt date or Northstar Receipt Date. Managed-pickup pickup alone does not start AP clock unless agreement says otherwise.
- Vendor-caused IER response normally due within 5 business days; after 10 business days without response, AP may reject or pay undisputed validated amount as appropriate.
- Exception ownership: price/terms Procurement; receipt quantity DC Operations; freight/routing Logistics; tax/banking/payment AP/Finance; safety/quality Q&R.
- VendorLink/Northstar connection outage >2 consecutive hours during time-sensitive window may receive temporary alternate-method approval; authoritative electronic record backfilled within 1 business day after restoration. Vendor-side outage is not an automatic waiver.
- Bank-account changes require secure VendorLink workflow + independent AP verification through an established contact/control. Email/invoice note/unverified phone instruction alone cannot change banking. AP may impose Payment Hold; Payment Hold does not itself change vendor status.
- Three vendor-controlled electronic transaction failures of same material type in rolling 60 days -> Vendor Compliance review. Deliberate duplicate billing, false invoice/receipt reference, or payment-redirection fraud may trigger immediate Payment Hold and vendor-status review.
- Transaction/AP records retained 7 years after final payment or final resolution unless longer rule applies; corrections preserve prior values, actor/system, timestamps, channel, and audit history.

Cross-file boundaries added by 09
- 10 owns dollar deductions, chargebacks, return/recall cost recovery, credit/offset appeal procedures, and formal appeals; 09 establishes invoice/payment facts and IER handling but not chargeback amounts.
- File 09 may display/link PO, APLR, ICR/SIP, Q&R, receiving, and SRR evidence without becoming authoritative for those underlying operational/technical decisions.



CANONICAL FACTS ESTABLISHED IN FILE 10
=======================================
Product action / returns data model
- New VendorLink records: Product Action Record (PAR), Routine Return Authorization (RRA), Chargeback Notice (CBN), and Appeal Case.
- Q&R owns safety classification, recall/withdrawal scope, holds, technical disposition, and technical closure. Financial review cannot override Q&R safety decisions.
- AP records credits/deductions; Finance controls final financial recovery/appeals; Vendor Compliance coordinates vendor execution.

Recall / withdrawal execution
- File 06 notification timing is preserved: urgent recall/stop-sale/serious-safety events within 4 business hours; other material regulatory/failed-safety-test events within 1 business day.
- For an urgent safety action, usable preliminary scope is due within the same 4-business-hour window when reasonably available; file 06 Traceability Challenge remains 4 hours when invoked.
- Unknown scope is not treated as zero scope; Q&R may control a broader population until evidence supports narrowing.
- Urgent actions drive hold/stop-sale, DC quarantine, in-transit control, and store/DC effectiveness checks.
- Northstar-controlled affected inventory must be reconciled to system-record units as quarantined, returned, destroyed, corrected/released, controlled in transit, previously sold, or documented variance.
- Financial appeals may remain open after PAR technical closure and never delay a safety action.

Routine return / disposition
- RRA vendor disposition instruction: 2 business days.
- Ordinary return pickup: 10 business days; refrigerated/frozen/perishable: 3 business days unless shorter direction applies.
- Vendor credit after documented receipt/approved destruction: 15 calendar days unless contract differs.
- Unsafe/recalled/controlled product cannot re-enter commerce merely because vendor requests pickup; Q&R may require destruction.

Chargeback / recovery controls
- CBN must identify NVID, linked PO/shipment/PAR/RER/SRR/IER, reason code, policy rule, event date, calculation, evidence, owner, issue date, appeal deadline.
- Same underlying cost cannot be recovered twice.
- Standard charges established: OTIF failure = 2% of affected late/short unit value (min $100, max $1,500/PO); DC No Show $200; manual ASN correction $75; unauthorized pallet/floor-load/SSCC handling $125; managed-pickup TONU = actual carrier charge + $75; routing/data incremental freight = actual + $100; confirmed vendor-responsible packaging/label/freshness/quality rework = $150 + documented incremental cost; import-document failure = $150 + documented direct import cost; vendor-responsible recall/withdrawal = documented reasonable direct execution cost + $250 PAR coordination charge.
- Government penalties pass through only when supplier responsibility plus agreement/law permit; broader indemnity/lost-sales/legal damages remain governed by contract/law.

Appeal controls
- First CBN appeal: 30 calendar days; recall/withdrawal direct-cost charge issued after PAR technical closure: 60 calendar days.
- First-level review target: 10 business days after complete appeal; one evidence request may give vendor 5 business days.
- Second-level appeal: within 10 calendar days after first decision; final decision target 15 business days.
- Final financial review: Finance Manager + Vendor Compliance Manager + owner of underlying factual record; Director Q&R participates when safety/quality/recall scope is disputed.
- Appeal does not automatically stay deduction. Timely first appeal >$2,500 may receive Finance Manager Deduction Hold only for credible material Northstar error/unresolved factual conflict; hold affects collection only.
- Formal vendor review triggers include 3 upheld standard chargebacks of same material type in rolling 60 days or aggregate upheld chargebacks >$5,000 in rolling 90 days, subject to stated isolated pass-through exception.
- File 06 CAP timing controls when the underlying issue is a quality finding.
- File 10 records retained 7 years after later of final financial resolution or PAR/RRA closure unless longer rule applies.
