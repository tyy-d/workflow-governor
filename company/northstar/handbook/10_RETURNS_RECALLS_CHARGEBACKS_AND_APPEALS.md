# Returns, Recalls, Chargebacks, and Appeals

## 1. Purpose and scope

This section governs Northstar Regional Markets procedures for merchandise returns, product recalls and withdrawals, stock recovery, disposition, supplier cost recovery, chargebacks, deductions, credits, and formal appeals. It applies to all Northstar vendors and all merchandise supplied to Northstar stores, Hudson Valley Distribution Center (HVDC), and Lehigh Valley Distribution Center (LVDC).

Applicable FDA, USDA-FSIS, CPSC, or other government recall/reporting requirements control. Northstar's classifications, deadlines, charges, and appeal rules below are company policy.

Quality & Regulatory (Q&R) owns safety classification, product holds, recall scope, and technical disposition. Vendor Compliance coordinates vendor execution and corrective action. Distribution Center Operations and Store Operations execute inventory control. Logistics coordinates reverse transportation. Accounts Payable (AP) records credits and deductions. Finance controls financial recovery and final commercial appeals.

A financial dispute never delays a Q&R safety hold, stop-sale, recall, withdrawal, quarantine, or legally required action.

## 2. Definitions

**Product Action Record (PAR):** VendorLink record for a recall, withdrawal, stock recovery, or other controlled product-removal event.

**Routine Return Authorization (RRA):** VendorLink authorization for non-recall merchandise return or vendor-directed disposition.

**Chargeback Notice (CBN):** VendorLink record stating a Northstar deduction or amount due, the policy basis, evidence, calculation, and appeal deadline.

**Appeal Case:** Formal VendorLink challenge to a CBN, return debit, or recall-cost recovery.

**Recall:** Product removal/correction treated as a recall by the responsible firm or applicable regulator. Northstar records any external recall classification separately and does not substitute an internal classification for an agency classification.

**Controlled Withdrawal:** Northstar removal of product from distribution or sale for a material quality, labeling, regulatory, or commercial issue that Q&R determines is not currently being handled as a recall.

**Stock Recovery:** Recovery of affected product while it remains under direct Northstar or supplier control and before ordinary sale/distribution to consumers.

## 3. Vendor responsibilities

Vendors must:

- maintain the 24/7 recall contact required under `00_COMPANY_AND_VENDOR_OVERVIEW.md`;
- immediately stop further shipment when the vendor knows product is subject to recall or a credible safety stop-distribution instruction;
- notify Northstar under the timelines in `06_QUALITY_AND_COMPLIANCE.md`;
- provide item, UPC/GTIN, lot/batch, date-code, manufacturing-site, quantity, distribution, hazard/reason, and regulator information sufficient to identify affected product;
- preserve relevant production, shipment, traceability, testing, and disposition records;
- follow Northstar quarantine/return/destruction instructions unless law or a government agency requires another action;
- issue requested credit or reimburse supplier-responsible documented costs; and
- submit disputes through VendorLink rather than withholding cooperation with a product action.

Vendors may not independently direct Northstar stores/DCs to return, destroy, relabel, release, or resume sale of controlled product.

## 4. Northstar product-action process

### 4.1 Initiation

A PAR may be opened from vendor notice, government notice, Q&R investigation, laboratory result, consumer complaint trend, recall of an ingredient/component, receiving evidence, or other credible information.

The notification deadlines already established in file 06 remain controlling:

| Event | Vendor notification to Northstar |
|---|---:|
| Active recall, stop-sale/stop-distribution, credible serious safety hazard, material government seizure/refusal, or confirmed falsified safety evidence | Within 4 business hours of learning |
| Other material regulatory notice, failed safety-critical test, certification suspension/withdrawal, or likely affected Northstar product | Within 1 business day |

For an urgent safety event, Q&R may impose a Northstar hold or stop-sale before complete vendor information is available.

### 4.2 Required PAR information

For an urgent safety action, a usable preliminary scope is due within the same 4-business-hour notification window when reasonably available. File 06's 4-hour Traceability Challenge applies when invoked.

Required data normally includes:

- NVID and vendor recall contact;
- Northstar item number and consumer GTIN/UPC;
- product description and pack size;
- affected and unaffected lot/date-code logic;
- manufacturing/packing facility;
- affected production dates;
- hazard, defect, misbranding, or withdrawal reason;
- regulator, case/reference number, and public notice if applicable;
- quantities shipped to Northstar by PO/shipment/location;
- in-transit quantity;
- proposed consumer remedy, if any;
- proposed return, destruction, correction, or other disposition; and
- status of related products using the same material, process, or site when relevant.

Unknown scope is not zero scope; Q&R may control a broader population until evidence supports narrowing it.

### 4.3 Internal execution

When Q&R activates an urgent safety PAR:

1. affected Northstar item/lot records are placed on hold;
2. Store Operations issues stop-sale instructions and, where technically available, activates point-of-sale blocking;
3. DC Operations quarantines affected saleable inventory and prevents allocation;
4. Logistics identifies in-transit loads and redirects or holds them as instructed;
5. Vendor Compliance tracks location acknowledgments and vendor responses; and
6. AP/Finance opens financial recovery tracking only after safety execution is underway.

Northstar-controlled inventory reconciliation must account for 100% of system-record affected units as one of: quarantined, returned, destroyed, corrected/released, in transit under control, previously sold, or documented inventory variance. Unresolved material variance remains open until Q&R accepts the explanation.

### 4.4 Effectiveness and closeout

For an urgent safety action, Vendor Compliance performs effectiveness checks against all affected Northstar DCs and stores rather than a sample. Lower-risk withdrawals may use a documented risk-based sample approved by Q&R.

PAR technical closure requires:

- affected Northstar-controlled inventory is reconciled;
- disposition evidence is complete;
- required consumer/store/DC communications are completed;
- regulator-required activity, if any, is not improperly represented as complete;
- financial recovery may be estimated or still open, but safety disposition is complete; and
- Q&R records technical closure.

Financial appeals may remain open after technical closure.

## 5. Routine returns and disposition

Routine rejection, excess inventory, discontinued merchandise, damaged goods, or other non-recall returns require an RRA unless the governing PO/agreement authorizes another process.

| Requirement | Northstar rule |
|---|---|
| Vendor disposition instruction after RRA request | 2 business days |
| Pickup of ordinary returnable merchandise | 10 business days after notice |
| Pickup of refrigerated/frozen/perishable returnable product | 3 business days unless Q&R/Logistics sets shorter |
| Credit after documented vendor receipt or approved destruction | 15 calendar days unless contract states otherwise |

If instructions are late, Northstar may choose a safe, commercially reasonable disposition and recover supplier-responsible costs. Unsafe or controlled product may not return to commerce merely because pickup is requested.

Acceptable disposition evidence may include signed return BOL, carrier proof of delivery, destruction certificate, recycler/salvage record where permitted, store/DC count, photographs, or other evidence Q&R/Vendor Compliance considers reliable.

## 6. Chargebacks and cost recovery

Chargebacks recover documented incremental cost and promote compliance. The same underlying cost may not be recovered twice; standard and actual-cost charges may coexist only for distinct impacts.

### 6.1 Standard chargeback schedule

| Event | Standard Northstar charge |
|---|---:|
| PO fails file 01 OTIF Pass | 2% of Northstar extended value of affected late/short units; minimum $100, maximum $1,500 per PO |
| DC No Show under file 07 | $200 per appointment |
| Missing/invalid required ASN that requires manual receiving correction | $75 per shipment |
| Unauthorized pallet/floor-load/SSCC configuration requiring manual handling | $125 per shipment |
| Vendor-caused Northstar-Managed Pickup truck ordered but freight not ready | Actual carrier TONU charge + $75 administration |
| Routing/BOL/weight/data failure causing incremental freight | Actual incremental freight + $100 administration |
| Confirmed vendor-responsible packaging/label/freshness/quality failure requiring DC/store rework | $150 per event + documented incremental rework/testing/disposition cost |
| Vendor-responsible import documentation failure | $150 processing + documented demurrage, detention, storage, broker correction, re-export, or destruction cost |
| Vendor-responsible recall/withdrawal | Documented reasonable direct execution cost + $250 incident-coordination charge per PAR |

No OTIF charge is duplicated merely because the same PO misses both the On-Time and In-Full thresholds. Government penalties are passed through only where supplier responsibility and applicable agreement/law permit.

### 6.2 Direct recall and return costs

Supplier-responsible recoverable costs may include reasonable documented:

- reverse freight;
- store/DC labor specifically required to locate, segregate, count, relabel, or remove affected goods;
- destruction or regulated disposal;
- third-party testing or inspection caused by the event;
- carrier detention/demurrage caused by supplier failure;
- consumer remedy amounts Northstar is obligated to fund on the supplier's behalf when contractually applicable; and
- outside professional services reasonably necessary for the event.

Lost sales, reputational/legal damages, and indemnification claims remain governed by contract and law.

## 7. Chargeback issuance and deductions

Each CBN must state:

- CBN number and NVID;
- affected PO/shipment/PAR/RER/SRR/IER references;
- chargeback reason code;
- policy rule;
- event date;
- calculation and amount;
- supporting evidence or evidence location;
- issuing department;
- issue date; and
- appeal deadline.

AP may deduct an approved CBN from amounts otherwise payable and must show the deduction on remittance or VendorLink payment detail. If no payable invoice balance exists, Northstar may issue a vendor debit request.

A financial deduction does not change PO performance facts, receiving disposition, facility status, Q&R finding severity, or vendor status unless the responsible policy owner separately changes those records.

## 8. Appeals

### 8.1 First appeal

A vendor must submit a complete Appeal Case within **30 calendar days** after CBN issue date. Recall/withdrawal direct-cost charges issued more than 30 days after PAR technical closure receive **60 calendar days** because supporting costs may be more complex.

An appeal must identify the disputed line/amount, requested remedy, factual basis, and evidence. Unsupported statements such as "carrier issue" or "not our fault" are insufficient when objective shipment, receipt, or system records should exist.

The issuing owner normally completes first-level review within **10 business days** after a complete appeal. Missing evidence may pause review once; the vendor then has **5 business days** to supply requested material.

### 8.2 Review authority

| Dispute subject | Primary factual owner |
|---|---|
| PO/OTIF | Vendor Compliance + Procurement |
| Receiving fact | DC Operations |
| Routing/carrier | Logistics |
| Import cost | Import Compliance Lead |
| Invoice/payment/deduction posting | AP |
| Safety, quality, recall scope, technical disposition | Q&R |
| Final financial recovery decision | Finance Manager |

Commercial reviewers cannot reverse Q&R safety determinations; new technical evidence returns to Q&R.

### 8.3 Second-level appeal

If the first appeal is denied in whole or part, the vendor has **10 calendar days** from the decision to request one final review. Final review requires the Finance Manager plus Vendor Compliance Manager and the manager owning the underlying factual record. Director, Quality & Regulatory participates when the disputed basis is safety/quality/recall scope.

Final written decision should be issued within **15 business days** after a complete second-level appeal. The decision must state upheld, reduced, reversed, or remanded for factual correction.

Appeal filing does not automatically suspend deduction. For a timely first appeal exceeding **$2,500**, the Finance Manager may place a temporary Deduction Hold when the evidence shows a credible material Northstar error or material unresolved factual conflict. Deduction Hold affects collection only, never product release or safety action.

## 9. Non-compliance and escalation

The following trigger formal vendor review:

- failure to notify Northstar of a known recall or serious safety action within required time;
- shipment or sale direction contrary to an active Northstar or government hold;
- deliberate narrowing or falsification of recall scope;
- failure to execute a required return/disposition after written notice;
- three upheld standard chargebacks of the same material type in rolling 60 days; or
- aggregate upheld chargebacks above $5,000 in a rolling 90 days, excluding isolated pass-through government/carrier charges not caused by a recurring vendor process weakness.

Safety concealment or falsification may support immediate Suspension under file 00. Corrective-action deadlines under file 06 control when the underlying event is classified as a quality finding.

## 10. Records and evidence

PAR, RRA, CBN, Appeal Case, disposition, recall-effectiveness, quantity-reconciliation, credit, deduction, and final-decision records are retained **7 years after the later of final financial resolution or PAR/RRA closure**, unless law, litigation hold, contract, or another Northstar policy requires longer.

Corrections preserve original values, timestamps, actors, reason, and evidence. Credits/appeals record the corrected outcome without erasing the event.

## 11. Examples / scenarios

**Recall scope uncertainty.** A supplier reports possible undeclared allergen exposure but cannot yet identify exact lots. Q&R opens a PAR and controls the broader potentially affected population. The vendor's appeal about potential lost sales cannot narrow the hold; technical evidence must first be accepted by Q&R.

**No-show charge.** A prepaid carrier never presents at HVDC and no approved reschedule exists by the file 07 threshold. Northstar issues a $200 CBN. The vendor provides a DockSchedule outage record showing a Northstar system failure. Vendor Compliance verifies it and reverses the chargeback without altering the underlying PO unless the file 01 PER criteria also support a performance correction.

**Recall cost dispute.** Northstar destroys recalled frozen food after Q&R determines return transport would create unacceptable risk. The CBN includes destruction and store labor evidence plus the $250 coordination charge. The vendor may dispute quantities or documented cost, but not order Northstar to release recalled product to avoid the deduction.

**Late appeal.** A $125 pallet charge is not challenged until 45 days after CBN issuance. No system-outage or notice-error evidence exists. The appeal is administratively closed as untimely without re-litigating the pallet facts.
