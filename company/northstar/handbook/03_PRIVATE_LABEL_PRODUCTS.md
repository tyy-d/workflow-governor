# Private Label Products

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs products sold under a Northstar-owned or controlled brand, or manufactured uniquely to a Northstar-approved formula, design, specification, or performance standard. It applies to food and non-food private-label products from development through item retirement. Under `00_COMPANY_AND_VENDOR_OVERVIEW.md`, all private-label foods are R3 Enhanced Food/Safety; non-food items retain the risk tier assigned by Quality & Regulatory (Q&R).

Merchandising owns the brand proposition and product intent. The **Private Brand Manager**, a role within Merchandising, coordinates private-label development. Q&R owns safety, regulatory, technical-specification, facility-approval, and release decisions. Procurement owns commercial commitments. Vendor Compliance coordinates supplier standing and corrective actions.

This file does not define detailed artwork, barcode, package-marking, import, laboratory, receiving, or recall procedures. Those requirements are controlled by the related handbook files listed in Section 11.

## 2. Definitions

**Private Label Product** — an item carrying a Northstar-owned or controlled mark, or an item made uniquely to a Northstar-controlled formula, specification, design, or performance standard.

**Private-Label Manufacturer** — the vendor or disclosed manufacturing entity that produces a Private Label Product for Northstar.

**Approved Manufacturing Site** — a physical production location recorded in Northstar VendorLink and approved for the specific product or product family before production begins.

**Private Label Technical File (PLTF)** — the authoritative VendorLink record for a private-label item. It links the approved specification, manufacturing site, regulatory and quality evidence, claims support, approved samples, freshness profile where applicable, and change history.

**Approved Product Specification (APS)** — the version-controlled technical requirements within the PLTF against which production is evaluated.

**Pre-Production Sample (PPS)** — a representative sample submitted before commercial production using the intended formulation/materials and substantially final consumer-facing presentation.

**First Production Lot (FPL)** — the first commercial-scale production lot after a new-item approval, approved manufacturing-site change, or other change designated by Q&R as requiring first-production verification.

**Material Change** — a change reasonably capable of affecting product identity, safety, regulatory status, consumer claims, quality, performance, traceability, shelf life, labeling, packaging compatibility, or Northstar brand expectations.

**Private Label Change Request (PLCR)** — the VendorLink record used to request approval for a planned Material Change. Emergency requests use the same record with the Emergency flag.

## 3. Vendor responsibilities

Private-label vendors must:

1. manufacture to the current APS and PLTF controls;
2. use only Approved Manufacturing Sites and disclosed subcontractors;
3. maintain risk-appropriate manufacturing and quality controls;
4. ensure materials, ingredients, components, processes, and finished goods support approved specifications and claims;
5. maintain lot-level traceability to site and production records;
6. obtain approval before a Material Change;
7. protect affected product when a failure, deviation, undeclared site, or questionable claim is identified;
8. provide complete, truthful technical information; and
9. use Northstar marks, designs, artwork, and specifications only for authorized Northstar production.

The seller of record remains responsible for compliance by all manufacturing facilities; outsourcing does not transfer Northstar obligations.

## 4. Northstar process

### 4.1 Development and site qualification

Before a private-label item may enter routine commercial production, the Private Brand Manager creates or sponsors a PLTF in VendorLink. The proposed manufacturing site must already be disclosed and must satisfy the facility requirements appropriate to the item's risk tier.

A commercial PO cannot authorize an unapproved site. R3 private-label food facilities must satisfy the file 00 food-safety baseline before Production Authorization.

### 4.2 Specification approval

The vendor submits product data sufficient to create an APS. The APS must define the characteristics needed to determine whether the finished product is the product Northstar approved. Depending on category, this may include formulation or bill of materials, ingredient/component requirements, dimensions or net content, sensory or performance targets, critical tolerances, applicable claims, storage condition, lot-code structure, and linked packaging requirements.

Merchandising defines consumer and brand expectations; Q&R determines technical, safety, and regulatory adequacy. Procurement may not bypass an unresolved Q&R requirement.

### 4.3 Pre-production approval

For a new private-label item, new Approved Manufacturing Site, or Material Change designated by Q&R, the vendor must submit the required PPS **at least 10 Northstar business days before the planned production start** unless VendorLink specifies a longer category lead time.

The PPS must use the intended formulation/materials and substantially final consumer-facing configuration. An artwork mock-up does not replace a required physical PPS.

Production may begin only after VendorLink shows **Production Authorized** for the applicable PLTF version and site. Production/Shipping Authorization are technical gates, not purchase commitments or PO revisions.

### 4.4 First-production verification and shipping release

The vendor must identify the FPL and upload required first-production evidence within **2 business days after lot completion**, including any records, samples, certificates, inspections, or laboratory results specified by Q&R.

For R3 private-label food and any other item marked **Hold for First-Production Release**, the FPL may not ship until VendorLink shows **Shipping Authorized**. For lower-risk items not marked for hold, the PLTF may permit shipment after Production Authorization provided all separately required testing and labeling approvals are current.

A successful PPS or first-production review does not authorize future deviations from the APS.

### 4.5 Routine production

After release, each lot must remain traceable to the current APS version, Approved Manufacturing Site, and PO. Northstar may compare samples, complaints, inspections, laboratory results, or store observations against the APS; Q&R resolves conflicting conformity evidence.

### 4.6 Change control

A vendor must submit a PLCR at least **60 calendar days before the planned production date** for a Material Change. Approval must be obtained before changed production begins, even if the vendor believes the change improves the product or does not affect cost.

If another Northstar policy requires earlier notice, the earlier deadline controls; freshness-related changes also remain subject to `02_PRODUCT_FRESHNESS.md`.

If an unplanned condition creates a Material Change or possible deviation, the vendor must notify Q&R and Vendor Compliance **within 1 business day of discovery** and before further affected shipment where reasonably possible. A PLCR does not itself authorize continued production or shipment.

## 5. Required documents and data

Each PLTF must contain or link to the evidence applicable to the item:

| Record | Minimum purpose |
|---|---|
| Approved Product Specification | Defines the authorized product and critical requirements |
| Approved Manufacturing Site | Identifies where production is permitted |
| Ingredient/formulation or component data | Supports identity, safety, regulatory review, and claims |
| Allergen declaration for applicable food | Supports allergen and label controls |
| Consumer-claim substantiation | Supports claims such as organic, kosher, non-GMO, performance, material, or similar representations |
| Approved PPS/reference evidence | Establishes the approved product presentation or benchmark |
| Q&R testing/inspection plan and current results | Shows required verification status; detailed rules are in file 06 |
| Artwork/label approval link | Shows consumer-facing information approval; details are in file 04 |
| Approved Freshness Profile, when applicable | Controls freshness under file 02 |
| Traceability/lot-code method | Connects finished goods to production records |
| PLCR and approval history | Provides controlled change history |
| Import/origin evidence, when applicable | Links to requirements in file 05 |

Missing evidence that is required for a release gate prevents that gate from being completed.

## 6. Standards and thresholds

### 6.1 Manufacturing-site control

Private-label production is permitted only at the Approved Manufacturing Site recorded for the active PLTF version. Moving all or part of finished-goods production to another site, including temporary overflow production, is a Material Change.

**Unauthorized finished-goods subcontracting is a Critical Private Label Deviation.** Affected product must be placed on hold, and Vendor Compliance and Q&R must review the vendor's status. Prior approval of the alternate site for another Northstar item does not authorize its use for the affected item.

### 6.2 Zero-tolerance controlled attributes

There is no commercial tolerance for an unauthorized change to:

- declared food allergens;
- product identity or legally required composition;
- manufacturing site;
- consumer claim requiring substantiation or certification;
- critical safety-related component or process identified in the APS;
- required regulatory certification status; or
- a Northstar-owned mark or mandatory consumer warning.

Other physical, sensory, dimensional, or performance attributes may have numerical tolerances in the APS. The APS, not informal historical variation, controls.

### 6.3 Annual PLTF recertification

For every active private-label item, the vendor must complete an **Annual Technical Attestation** in VendorLink once every **12 months**, confirming that the manufacturing site, formulation/components, claims, specification, and material production conditions remain accurate.

VendorLink opens the attestation **30 calendar days before** the anniversary. If the attestation is not completed by the anniversary, the PLTF becomes **Technical Review Due**. If it remains incomplete for **15 calendar days after** the anniversary, new PO production may not begin until Vendor Compliance and Q&R clear the record. Existing finished goods are not automatically unsafe or rejected solely because an administrative attestation is late.

### 6.4 Material-change classification

Material Changes normally include:

- formulation, ingredient, or major component changes;
- an ingredient or component supplier change that could affect allergen, safety, regulated composition, certified status, or a consumer claim;
- manufacturing-site or finished-goods subcontractor changes;
- process changes capable of affecting safety, identity, shelf life, or performance;
- direct-food-contact or other functionally critical packaging-material changes;
- net content, count, size, or product configuration changes;
- new or revised consumer claims;
- country-of-origin changes;
- shelf-life basis or storage-condition changes; and
- traceability/date-code system changes.

Purely administrative corrections that cannot affect the product or consumer-facing information may be treated as non-material by the owning Northstar team. When classification is ambiguous, Q&R has final authority.

### 6.5 Claims and certifications

The PLTF must contain evidence appropriate to each consumer claim before Q&R and Merchandising approve it. Expired or withdrawn certification evidence suspends the related claim authorization until corrected. Label placement and artwork controls belong to `04_PACKAGING_AND_LABELING.md`.

## 7. Exceptions and escalation

An emergency PLCR may be requested for a credible supply disruption, facility emergency, regulatory change, or similar event. It must identify affected items/POs/lots, the proposed change, evidence, risk, duration, and restoration plan.

| Exception type | Minimum Northstar approval |
|---|---|
| Non-safety aesthetic or performance deviation within a defined temporary scope | Private Brand Manager + Q&R approval |
| Emergency material/component substitution with no unresolved safety or regulatory issue | Procurement Manager + Private Brand Manager + Q&R approval |
| R3 food formulation, allergen, safety-process, or manufacturing-site change | Director, Quality & Regulatory + Private Brand Manager; Procurement Manager if commercial supply is affected |
| Shipping product while a private-label Q&R hold is active | Director, Quality & Regulatory only where the underlying condition can lawfully and safely be resolved; otherwise prohibited |

No exception may waive applicable law, authorize a knowingly unsafe product, conceal an undeclared allergen, validate falsified evidence, or retroactively legitimize production at an undisclosed site. No-expiry or blanket private-label exceptions are invalid.

Q&R may require samples, testing, facility evidence, or expert review before deciding whether a proposed change is genuinely equivalent.

## 8. Non-compliance

Private-label non-compliance may result in production stop, Shipping Hold, lot rejection, testing, corrective action, item restriction, Restricted status, suspension, or later cost recovery.

Immediate escalation is required for unauthorized manufacturing sites, deliberate specification substitution, falsified technical or certification records, concealed test failures, unauthorized Northstar-brand production, or knowingly inaccurate allergen/safety information. These events may trigger Suspension under the integrity rules established in file 00.

A vendor may not ship a known nonconforming private-label product merely to protect OTIF performance. Any performance treatment is determined under `01_PURCHASE_ORDERS_AND_DELIVERY_PERFORMANCE.md`; safety and product authorization take precedence over score preservation.

## 9. Records and evidence

Records must allow Northstar to reconstruct which approved version governed any private-label lot, including:

- PLTF and historical APS versions;
- manufacturing-site approval history;
- PPS and first-production evidence;
- Q&R release decisions;
- applicable test, inspection, certificate, and claim-support records;
- annual technical attestations;
- PLCRs, emergency requests, approvals, and denials;
- lot/manufacturing genealogy sufficient to link affected product to site and production records; and
- material correspondence and corrective actions.

Northstar retains PLTF approval/change/release records for **7 years after the later of the item's retirement date or last Northstar receipt**, unless a longer period applies because of law, recall, litigation hold, contract, or another policy.

## 10. Examples / scenarios

### Scenario A — alternate factory for capacity

A private-label household-goods supplier cannot meet demand and proposes moving one PO to a factory that already makes another Northstar item. The alternate factory is not approved for this item. Production cannot begin there until a PLCR is approved and the site/product authorization is updated. Producing first and requesting approval afterward is an unauthorized-site deviation.

### Scenario B — ingredient supplier change

A private-label cookie manufacturer changes the source of a flavoring. The formula name appears unchanged, but the new source may have a different allergen cross-contact profile. The vendor must treat the change as material and obtain approval before affected production. Commercial urgency does not permit an allergen-related bypass.

### Scenario C — first R3 production lot

A new refrigerated private-label dip has an approved APS, AFP, site, and artwork. The first commercial lot is produced, but required first-production evidence is still under Q&R review. Because R3 private-label food is subject to first-production shipping release, the lot may not ship until VendorLink shows Shipping Authorized.

### Scenario D — missed annual attestation

A private-label shelf-stable item reaches its technical anniversary with no vendor attestation. The PLTF becomes Technical Review Due. If the vendor is still incomplete 15 days later, new production for Northstar POs cannot start until Q&R and Vendor Compliance clear the technical record; already received product is not automatically rejected solely for the late attestation.
