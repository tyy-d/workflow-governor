# Northstar Regional Markets — Company and Vendor Overview

> **Fictional policy environment.** Northstar Regional Markets ("Northstar") is a fictional company created for Workflow Governor testing. Northstar policies in this handbook are original fictional requirements. References to real organizations in the `Reference basis` section identify public materials used only as structural or operational inspiration.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This section establishes vendor classifications, onboarding status, minimum information requirements, decision ownership, and the records required before merchandise vendors may transact with Northstar. Northstar operates approximately 35 stores and two regional distribution centers serving New York, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey. Its assortment includes packaged, refrigerated, frozen, and fresh foods plus household, personal-care, private-label, and limited imported merchandise.

It applies to manufacturers, brand owners, distributors, seller-of-record brokers, import vendors, and approved direct-store-delivery suppliers. Non-merchandise service providers are outside this handbook unless another section expressly applies.

Policy ownership is shared as follows:

| Function | Baseline responsibility |
|---|---|
| Procurement | Commercial relationship, vendor selection, negotiated terms |
| Merchandising | Assortment decision, item authorization, product positioning |
| Vendor Compliance | Onboarding administration, vendor standing, policy exceptions, corrective-action coordination |
| Quality & Regulatory | Food safety, product safety, regulatory documentation, facility approval, safety holds |
| Logistics | Network routing and inbound logistics standards |
| Distribution Center Operations | Physical receiving and shipment evidence |
| Accounts Payable (AP) | Tax, banking, remittance, invoice-payment setup |
| Store Operations | Store-level execution and direct-store-delivery feedback |
| Finance | Financial controls and material commercial-risk review |
| IT | Vendor connectivity and security requirements for Northstar systems |

## 2. Definitions and vendor classification

### 2.1 Core terms

- **Vendor** — the legal entity from which Northstar purchases merchandise.
- **Seller of record** — the entity named on the purchase order and invoice and financially responsible for the transaction.
- **Manufacturing site** — a facility that manufactures, processes, packs, repacks, or materially alters a Northstar product.
- **Approved item** — a specific product authorized by Merchandising and, when applicable, Quality & Regulatory.
- **Northstar VendorLink** — Northstar's vendor-facing portal for onboarding, compliance documents, item submissions, notices, exception requests, and selected financial records.
- **NVID** — the unique Northstar Vendor ID assigned to a legal vendor entity after initial onboarding review.
- **Commercial shipment** — merchandise shipped against a Northstar purchase order for resale; samples and approved testing units are not commercial shipments.

### 2.2 Vendor role

Each vendor record has one primary seller-of-record role.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Brand Supplier | Sells branded merchandise it manufactures or controls |
| Private-Label Manufacturer | Manufactures merchandise sold under a Northstar-owned or Northstar-controlled brand |
| Distributor / Broker-Seller | Resells products from one or more manufacturers and is seller of record |
| Import Vendor | Is seller of record for merchandise imported into the United States, whether or not another party acts as customs broker |
| Direct-Store-Delivery (DSD) Supplier | Delivers approved products directly to Northstar stores under an approved DSD program |

A broker that is not seller of record is a commercial representative, not the Northstar vendor.

### 2.3 Product Risk Tier

Risk tier is assigned at the **item/facility level** by Quality & Regulatory. A vendor's displayed risk tier is the highest tier among active items it supplies.

| Tier | Typical products | Baseline oversight |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 — Standard** | Non-food, nonhazardous general merchandise with limited regulatory complexity | Standard onboarding and item-data review |
| **R2 — Controlled** | Shelf-stable foods, personal-care products, household chemical products, or other regulated/label-sensitive goods | Enhanced specification and compliance review |
| **R3 — Enhanced Food/Safety** | Refrigerated or frozen foods, fresh foods, ready-to-eat foods with material food-safety controls, private-label foods, or items designated high-risk by Quality & Regulatory | Facility-level food-safety review and enhanced documentation |

Quality & Regulatory may raise an item's tier when its process, intended use, regulatory status, complaint history, recall history, or manufacturing environment creates greater risk. Merchandising and Procurement may not lower a risk tier.

## 3. Vendor responsibilities

Every vendor must provide truthful legal, tax, banking, contact, facility, and item information; comply with applicable law; supply only approved items from approved sites; maintain traceability and investigation records; keep insurance and required licenses/certifications current; obtain required approval before controlled changes; protect Northstar information; and cooperate with audits, recalls, investigations, and corrective actions. Vendors remain responsible when they use co-manufacturers, warehouses, carriers, brokers, laboratories, or other third parties.

## 4. Northstar vendor lifecycle

### 4.1 Status model

Northstar VendorLink records one of the following statuses for each NVID:

| Status | Meaning | Commercial PO / shipment rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Applicant** | Sourcing discussion has begun; onboarding is incomplete | No commercial POs; samples only if requested |
| **Conditional** | Core identity review passed, but one or more activation requirements remain | No commercial POs; testing, data setup, and sample activity allowed |
| **Active** | Vendor is approved to transact for at least one authorized item | Commercial POs permitted for approved items |
| **Restricted** | A remediable compliance or administrative issue exists | New PO release blocked unless a written exception is approved; existing orders handled case-by-case |
| **Suspended** | Material safety, integrity, legal, or repeated-performance concern exists | No new POs and no shipments unless the suspension notice expressly authorizes a controlled action |
| **Closed** | Relationship ended or vendor inactive by decision | No commercial activity |

### 4.2 Normal onboarding sequence

1. **Sourcing nomination.** Procurement or Merchandising creates the applicant record and identifies intended product categories.
2. **Legal/entity validation.** Vendor Compliance confirms seller-of-record identity; AP validates tax and payment setup.
3. **Risk classification.** Quality & Regulatory assigns preliminary Product Risk Tier based on proposed items and facilities.
4. **Document review.** Required onboarding evidence is checked for completeness and validity.
5. **Facility/product review.** Additional Quality & Regulatory review is completed when R2/R3 rules require it.
6. **System enablement.** Vendor completes VendorLink access setup and required purchase-order/invoice connectivity testing.
7. **Item authorization.** Merchandising authorizes the item; Quality & Regulatory approval is also required when applicable.
8. **Activation.** Vendor Compliance changes the NVID to Active after all activation gates are satisfied.

A sourcing discussion, sample request, NVID, forecast, or verbal commitment never authorizes commercial shipment.

## 5. Required documents and data

The following are baseline onboarding requirements. Later handbook sections may add item-, facility-, import-, or private-label-specific documents.

| Record | Applies to | Minimum rule |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor Profile | All vendors | Legal name, entity type, addresses, contacts, ownership/control information requested by Northstar |
| Tax Form | All vendors | Current signed Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8 series, as appropriate |
| Northstar Merchandise Terms acknowledgment | All vendors | Executed/accepted before activation |
| Banking / payment setup | All vendors receiving electronic payment | Independently validated by AP before payment activation |
| Certificate of Insurance (COI) | All commercial merchandise vendors | Must meet Northstar limits and remain unexpired |
| Primary + backup operational contacts | All vendors | At minimum commercial, logistics, AP, and compliance contacts; food vendors also require a 24/7 recall contact |
| Manufacturing / packing site list | Vendors whose products are manufactured or packed for Northstar | Site name, address, activity, and applicable regulatory identifiers |
| Item master submission | Each proposed item | UPC/GTIN where applicable, case pack, dimensions, weights, cost, descriptions, images, and category-specific fields |
| Ingredient / allergen / specification information | Food items | Current information sufficient for Northstar item and safety review |
| Food-safety audit or certification evidence | R3 suppliers and other vendors when specifically required | Current facility-level evidence accepted by Quality & Regulatory |
| Required regulatory license/registration attestation | Regulated products/facilities | Vendor identifies applicable regulator and current status; Northstar may request supporting evidence |

### 5.1 Food-specific baseline

Food vendors must identify applicable FDA food-facility registration/preventive-controls status or USDA-FSIS establishment identity when relevant. Northstar policy, not federal law, requires **R3 manufacturing facilities** to maintain either (a) a current certification against an appropriate GFSI-recognized certification programme or (b) a written Northstar-approved alternative audit plan. Detailed acceptance rules belong in `06_QUALITY_AND_COMPLIANCE.md`. Food-item data must also identify ingredients and major allergens as applicable; this does not replace the vendor's legal labeling duties.

## 6. Standards and thresholds

### 6.1 Activation gates

Vendor Compliance may set an NVID to **Active** only when applicable VendorLink gates are `PASS`: seller identity, tax/payment validation, Merchandise Terms, current COI, required contacts, risk classification, required facility documents, no critical Quality & Regulatory block, connectivity test (or approved exception), and at least one authorized item.

### 6.2 Data and document maintenance

- Vendor master changes must be submitted within **10 business days** after the vendor knows of a change, unless another policy requires advance notice.
- Changes to legal name, tax identity, payment account, manufacturing site, product formulation, allergen profile, pack/size, or other controlled product data must follow the applicable change-approval process **before** the changed condition is used for Northstar business.
- Vendor contacts must be recertified in VendorLink **annually**.
- A COI expiring within **30 calendar days** creates a warning. If the COI expires without an accepted replacement, the vendor becomes **Restricted** automatically at 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on the day after expiration.
- An R3 facility whose required food-safety certification or approved alternative audit expires becomes **Restricted for items produced at that facility** unless Quality & Regulatory has approved a time-limited exception before expiration.

### 6.3 Insurance baseline — Northstar policy

Unless a written category-specific requirement is higher, commercial merchandise vendors must maintain:

- Commercial General Liability: **$2,000,000 per occurrence** and **$4,000,000 aggregate**;
- Product Liability, whether separate or included in CGL: **$2,000,000 per occurrence**;
- Automobile Liability: **$1,000,000 combined single limit** when the vendor or its controlled fleet delivers to Northstar; and
- Workers' compensation as legally required for the vendor's operations.

These limits are Northstar contractual policy, not a statement of statutory minimum insurance requirements.

## 7. Exceptions and escalation

Exceptions must be recorded in VendorLink with the affected NVID/item/facility, policy rule, business justification, supporting evidence, approver, start date, and expiration date.

| Exception type | Minimum approval |
|---|---|
| Missing non-safety administrative item for ≤30 days | Vendor Compliance Manager + owning business manager |
| Temporary transaction/connectivity exception | Vendor Compliance Manager + AP or IT, depending on issue |
| Food-safety certification alternative / lapse | Director, Quality & Regulatory |
| Shipping while vendor is Restricted | Vendor Compliance Manager plus the function that owns the restricting condition |
| Any exception involving known product-safety risk | Director, Quality & Regulatory; additional executive review may be required |

No Northstar employee may waive a legal or regulatory requirement. Procurement and Merchandising cannot override a Quality & Regulatory safety hold. An exception that lacks a recorded expiration date is invalid.

## 8. Non-compliance

Northstar may use progressively stronger controls depending on severity, recurrence, evidence, and customer risk. Possible actions include document hold, item hold, vendor Restriction, shipment rejection, corrective-action request, chargeback where another policy authorizes one, enhanced audit, temporary Suspension, or relationship termination.

A **critical integrity issue**—including falsified certificates, concealed manufacturing sites, knowingly false allergen information, shipment during a stated safety suspension, or material misrepresentation during a recall investigation—may trigger immediate Suspension without progressive steps.

Safety actions are preventive controls, not findings of vendor fault. Commercial disputes over cost or responsibility are resolved separately from any action needed to protect customers.

## 9. Records and evidence

Each onboarding/status workflow must leave a reviewable VendorLink evidence package containing the NVID/legal entity, role/status, risk tier, tax/payment validation, COI, accepted Terms version, contacts, facility approval state, applicable compliance/audit documents, item authorizations, connectivity result or exception, corrective actions, and the date/user/reason for each status change.

Northstar retains vendor onboarding, approval, exception, and status-history records for **seven years after the NVID is Closed**, unless law, litigation hold, recall needs, or another Northstar policy requires longer retention. Product traceability and transactional retention rules may differ and are defined in the relevant later sections.

## 10. Examples / scenarios

### Scenario A — new shelf-stable branded food supplier
A cereal supplier completes tax, COI, contact, facility, specification, allergen, and connectivity requirements. The item is R2 with no additional facility gate. After Merchandising approval, Vendor Compliance may activate the vendor.

### Scenario B — refrigerated private-label supplier missing audit evidence
A refrigerated private-label manufacturer lacks current accepted R3 audit evidence. Its NVID remains Conditional; an approaching launch date does not permit Procurement to release a commercial PO.

### Scenario C — mixed-category distributor
A distributor supplies R1 household goods and an R3 refrigerated item, so its dashboard displays R3. A facility-specific restriction on the refrigerated item does not block unrelated R1 items unless the notice restricts the entire legal entity.

### Scenario D — insurance expiration
A vendor's COI expires June 30 without an accepted replacement. VendorLink changes it to Restricted July 1, and a July 2 PO request is blocked unless an authorized exception exists. Upload alone does not clear the block; the COI must be accepted.

### Scenario E — undisclosed manufacturing-site change
A food vendor moves production to an unapproved co-manufacturer. The affected item is held even if formulation is unchanged. Quality & Regulatory determines evidence required for release.
