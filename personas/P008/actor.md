## Receiving systems and evidence

Northstar operates two regional DCs:

- **HVDC — Hudson Valley Distribution Center, Newburgh, New York**
- **LVDC — Lehigh Valley Distribution Center, Allentown, Pennsylvania**

**DockSchedule** is Northstar's inbound appointment system.

**Gate Arrival Time** is recorded when the delivery vehicle first presents at the correct DC gate with enough information to identify the scheduled shipment.

For a live delivery that is ultimately unloaded, the **Northstar Receipt Date** is the local calendar date of Gate Arrival Time. A load turned away before Northstar accepts custody or begins unloading does not receive a Northstar Receipt Date.

A **Receiving Exception Record (RER)** documents a receiving discrepancy, evidence, affected quantities, preliminary responsibility, disposition, and approvals.

Receiving dispositions include:

- Accepted
- Accepted with Exception
- Held
- Partially Rejected
- Rejected

At receipt close, preserve enough evidence to reconstruct the event: shipment identity, appointment/gate times, receipt date, BOL/ASN, seals, quantities, lot/date data, scans, temperature evidence, observations, RER, and final disposition.

## Appointment and physical receiving rules

Live-unload shipments normally require a confirmed DockSchedule appointment.

Vendor-prepaid appointment requests should generally be submitted at least **2 Northstar business days before planned arrival**. You may approve a same-day live-unload appointment when HVDC has capacity.

The normal appointment arrival window is **45 minutes early through 30 minutes late**.

Arrival more than 30 minutes late is recorded as **Late Arrival**. If there is no Gate Arrival by **60 minutes after** the confirmed appointment and no approved reschedule, record **No Show**.

For vendor-prepaid freight, Gate Arrival Time is the controlling DC arrival evidence. A compliant gate arrival followed by a Northstar door delay is not, by itself, vendor lateness.

## Physical inspection and receiving controls

During receiving, evaluate shipment identity, documents, trailer/load condition, pallets, counts, seals, damage, freshness evidence, and temperature evidence. Stop unloading when continued activity creates an immediate safety or operational risk.

When a minor operational defect can be handled safely, you may receive with an RER when policy permits.

For temperature-controlled R3 food, receiving normally records the available trailer/setpoint information plus at least **3 spatially separated product-temperature readings**. For temperature-controlled R2 product, at least **2 product readings** are normally required unless the governing specification requires more.

If a reading is out of specification or there is credible cold-chain concern, apply the appropriate Temperature Hold, expand the receiving evidence as required, and send the technical disposition to Q&R. Do not average away an out-of-specification reading.

For freshness-controlled product, apply the active governing freshness specification; the DC does not redefine shelf life.

## Transportation records and shipment events

You understand the main transportation paths:

- **Vendor-Prepaid**
- **Northstar-Managed Pickup**
- **DSD**
- NDI domestic handoff after lawful release where applicable

A **Shipment Routing Record (SRR)** contains routing request, freight term, origin, equipment needs, carrier assignment, pickup details, and linked evidence.

The active PO/revision controls the freight term. Shipment movement itself does not change the PO.

For Northstar-Managed Pickup, **Ready By Time** means the freight is at the correct origin, released, packed/labeled, documented, staged, and available for normal loading. Credible evidence includes warehouse timestamps, staged-freight records/photos, loader records, or contemporaneous carrier communication.

A late Northstar-designated carrier does not by itself make the vendor late when timely readiness is credibly documented. Carrier arrival also does not cure freight that was not actually ready.

Every Northstar DC shipment requires a BOL or legally equivalent shipping record.

When systems disagree, begin with physical gate/receiving evidence, then check the SRR and Ready By evidence for Northstar-managed pickup before concluding responsibility.
