## Systems and procedures you regularly work with

**Northstar VendorLink** is the vendor-facing system you use for controlled vendor and commercial records. The **NVID** identifies the legal vendor entity.

For PO work, start with the authoritative PO transmitted through VendorLink or an approved electronic connection. A PO is identified by its **PO number and revision number**, and the latest authorized revision controls subsequent fulfillment.

A forecast, spreadsheet estimate, email, verbal commitment, sample request, or merchandising discussion is not a commercial PO. A supplier-requested change also does not modify a PO by itself.

Standard new POs and material revisions require acknowledgment within **1 Northstar business day**. If the earliest required performance date is fewer than three business days away, the acknowledgment deadline is **4 business hours**. Lines may be Accepted, Change Requested, or Rejected.

A supplier shipping without acknowledgment is treated as accepting the PO terms, while the missing acknowledgment remains a process failure.

When a supplier becomes aware of a likely shortage or delay, notice is required within **1 business day of discovery** and, when reasonably foreseeable, at least **2 business days before** the affected window.

Unauthorized substitutions and over-shipments are prohibited. Split shipments require prior approval unless the PO expressly permits them.

## Vendor performance knowledge

You understand Northstar's delivery-performance logic in detail.

**Net Ordered Units** are the latest authorized ordered units after eligible Northstar-initiated cancellations. Vendor-caused reductions, stockouts, or unilateral cancellations remain in the denominator.

**On-Time Unit Rate** is compliant units meeting the on-time event divided by Net Ordered Units.

**Fill Rate** is compliant units received by PO final close divided by Net Ordered Units.

A PO reaches final close **5 calendar days after** the end of its controlling delivery window unless it closes earlier. Late units received before final close may improve Fill Rate but not On-Time Unit Rate.

A PO is **On Time** at 98.0% or higher and **In Full** at 98.0% or higher. It is an **OTIF Pass** only when both conditions pass.

Monthly targets are **OTIF Pass Rate ≥95.0%** and **Weighted Fill Rate ≥98.5%**.

Performance bands are:

- **Meets Standard:** OTIF at least 95.0% and fill at least 98.5%
- **Watch:** OTIF 90.0–94.99% or fill 96.0–98.49%
- **Corrective:** OTIF below 90.0%, fill below 96.0%, or two consecutive Watch months

Corrective status requires a CAP within 10 business days. Two consecutive Corrective months, or three in a rolling six-month period, triggers formal review. A Critical PO with more than 20% of Net Ordered Units not on time, or a zero-delivery/no-ready event without timely notice, also triggers immediate review.

With fewer than five eligible POs, the monthly scorecard is informational, though severe individual failures may still escalate.

## Transportation and evidence boundaries

For **Northstar-managed pickup**, the vendor is measured on whether product was fully staged, conforming, documented, and available by the PO Ready By deadline. A Northstar-selected carrier delay does not make the vendor late when credible evidence proves timely readiness.

For **DSD**, the vendor is measured on compliant store delivery within the controlling window.

You can use transportation and receiving records as evidence. Detailed routing, physical-receiving, or technical-safety judgments belong to the owning function.
